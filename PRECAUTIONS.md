# WhatsApp Bulk Sender - Precautions & Guidelines

## Anti-Blocking Features Built-In

This app includes the following protections:

1. **Random Delays** - Messages are sent with random delays (not fixed intervals) to appear natural
2. **Auto-Pause** - If 5 consecutive messages fail, sending stops automatically (possible ban detected)
3. **Message Variation** - Each message has a unique invisible character to avoid duplicate detection
4. **WhatsApp Number Check** - Verifies each number exists on WhatsApp before sending

## Best Practices for Marriage Invitations

### Before Sending
1. **Warm up your number** - Use it normally for calls, chats for 1-2 weeks before bulk sending
2. **Save all contacts** - Make sure all recipients are saved in your phone
3. **Test first** - Send a test message to yourself or 2-3 friends first
4. **Use media** - Send an invitation image with text (looks more personal)

### During Sending
1. **Start small** - Send to 20-30 contacts first, wait 1 hour, then continue
2. **Spread over days** - If you have 300+ guests, send 100-150 per day
3. **Use working hours** - Send between 9 AM and 8 PM only
4. **Keep delays 5-10 seconds** - Don't go below 5 seconds

### After Sending
1. **Monitor delivery** - Check for failed messages
2. **Follow up manually** - Call important guests who didn't receive
3. **Wait before next batch** - At least 2-3 hours between batches

## Warning Signs of Blocking

If you see these, **STOP sending immediately**:
- Messages stuck at single check mark (sent but not delivered)
- Can't send messages to non-contacts
- QR code keeps disconnecting
- "This account can't send messages" error
- Business features restricted

---

## Recovery if Blocked

### Temporary Restriction (24-72 hours)
1. Stop all bulk sending
2. Use the app normally for personal chats
3. Wait 24-72 hours before retrying

### Account Ban
1. Contact WhatsApp support through the app
2. Submit an appeal explaining it's for personal use (marriage)
3. Wait for response (usually 24-48 hours)

## Do's and Don'ts

### DO:
- Personalize messages with guest names
- Use invitation images/media
- Send during daytime hours
- Keep contacts saved on your phone
- Use an established phone number

### DON'T:
- Send identical messages to everyone (the app adds variation automatically)
- Send to numbers not saved in your phone
- Send after 10 PM or before 8 AM
- Rush by setting delay below 5 seconds
- Send to 500+ people in one day with a new number
